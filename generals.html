Boss Compendium

The Five Cursed Generals

ELEMENT: WIND

GENERAL I

Ryū-no-Kaze

"The Wind That Cuts Before It Arrives"

Blade of Wind
The Appearance

Tall and impossibly lean, Ryū moves like smoke. Wears a tattered blue-grey haori that billows even with no wind. His face is half-covered by a cracked ivory mask. The Shadow Blade at his hip hums constantly.

Combat Techniques

Ghost Step

Teleports behind you using a burst of wind. First two hits give no audio cue.

Vacuum Slash

Sends an invisible wind blade. Only visible by the grass it cuts.

The Arena

A cliff-top bridge at dusk. Wind so strong it pushes Kael toward the edge mid-fight. Watch the grass, not the man.

ELEMENT: FIRE

GENERAL II

Kazan

"The Mountain That Burns"

Blade of Fire
The Appearance

Enormous, built like a boulder. Scorched black armor that glows orange at the seams. His eyes are permanently ember-red from the curse.

Combat Techniques

Eruption Slam

Causes a ring of fire to explode outward. Jump or get knocked into lava.

Magma Armor

Immune to normal hits. Must use wind-stance to crack his shell first.

The Arena

Inside an active volcano forge. The floor cracks open revealing lava in Phase 2.

ELEMENT: POISON

GENERAL III

Yomi

"She Who Walks Through the Dying"

Blade of Poison
The Appearance

Slender and eerily graceful. White hair and layered green-black silk robes that leave toxic mist on the ground. Hidden wakizashi.

Combat Techniques

Widow's Kiss

A precise strike that poisons you. Must destroy her hidden orbs to cure.

Mirage Step

Creates 3 clones. Use Soul Sight to find the one that casts a shadow.

The Arena

A moonlit poison garden. Beautiful cherry blossoms hide lethal toxic mists.

ELEMENT: ILLUSION

GENERAL IV

Shira the Hollow

"You Cannot Strike What Isn't There"

Blade of Illusion
The Appearance

Unsettlingly calm. White mask, violet kimono. Their body flickers like a candle, existing halfway between worlds.

Combat Techniques

The Lie

False attack animations. Learn the real tell: a slight shoulder drop.

Unmaking

Phase 2: Flips the arena upside down. Gravity reverses for Kael.

The Unique Mechanic

Close your eyes. Use audio cues to block real strikes while ignoring visual clones.

ELEMENT: SHADOW

GENERAL V

Mugen

"He Was You, Once"

Blade of Shadow
The Appearance

A mirror image of Kael, but in white robes with hollow black eyes. The first survivor who chose power over honor.

Combat Techniques

Dark Mirror

Copies and counters every ability you have unlocked. Don't fight like him.

The Becoming

Offers you the blade. Refuse it to trigger the true final phase.

The Arena

The ruins of the Kurogane clan forge. Your old home—now only ash and bone.
